# MEMAR demo video (target 2:45, hard limit 3:00)

Record the **Lens Studio Preview** (OBS or Win+G, 1080p). Talk over it, or record your voice after. Upload to YouTube as **Public** (the rules say public; Unlisted is risky).

Before recording:
- Select **MemoraApp** in the Scene Hierarchy and tick **resetOnStart**, so every take starts from the demo seed (8 memories). Untick it after recording.
- Make sure you have internet (voice capture uses Snap's speech-to-text) and your laptop mic works. **Turn on the Preview microphone:** the mic icon at the bottom-left of the Preview panel must not have a line through it.
- Close other windows, hide notifications. Do one full practice run.

In Interactive Preview: WASD moves, Q/E down/up, right-drag looks around, a click is a pinch.
