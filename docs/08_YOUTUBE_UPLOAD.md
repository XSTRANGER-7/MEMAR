MEMAR: reminders that live on your things (SPECS Lens, Learn AR with SPECS)

**Description**
MEMAR (memory + AR) is a Lens for SPECS that puts reminders on the things they're about. Your keys say "collect the parcel". The medicine box says "vitamin D after lunch". The front door checks what you carry before you leave. Look at an object, or pinch it, and its memories open right beside it.

Try it on your phone: https://xstranger-7.github.io/MEMAR/ (web simulator, same memory engine; tap = pinch)
Code and the Lens: https://github.com/XSTRANGER-7/MEMAR

The opening scenes are an illustrated concept of how MEMAR could feel on real glasses. Everything after that is the real Lens running in Lens Studio's SPECS preview, and a real-time recording of the web simulator.

Built with CLAD and the Lens Studio MCP. 14 / 14 LEAF scenarios and 41 engine tests pass.
